About Massage Dubai and how we check centres

Updated 1 October 2026.

Massage Dubai is an independent guide written for people who live in Dubai: expats who have just arrived and are working out where to go, long-time residents who want to try something new, and Emirati families looking for a trusted address. We explain what each type of massage actually involves, who it suits, what it typically costs in AED and where you can book it.

How we choose the centres we list

Every type page lists at least four centres that clearly offer that treatment on their current menu. We look for a spread of neighbourhoods and budgets, from five-star hotel spas to neighbourhood clinics, and we prefer specialists: an Ayurveda clinic for Ayurvedic massage, a physiotherapy or sports clinic for sports and medical massage, a hospital or qualified instructor for baby massage classes.

We only list licensed businesses with a fixed address or an established home-service company. We leave out anything advertised through cards on windscreens, anonymous WhatsApp numbers or classified ads, and any business whose reviews or advertising suggest services that are not part of professional massage therapy.

How we verify details

Each name, address and phone number is checked against the centre's official website or, where that is not possible, a reputable listing such as the hotel group's own page or a mall directory. Phone numbers are written in full international format so you can dial them from any phone. We do not link out to the centres and nobody pays to appear. Details were last checked in October 2026. If something has changed, please let us know so we can correct it.

Our editorial approach

We write about massage as a wellness service, not a cure. Where research supports a benefit we say so, and where a claim belongs to tradition rather than evidence we say that too. Nothing on this site is medical advice: if you are pregnant, have a heart condition, a recent injury, a blood-clotting disorder or take regular medication, check with your doctor before booking.

Prices are given as typical ranges because they change often in Dubai, especially around Ramadan, the summer offers season and the cooler winter months. Treat them as a guide and confirm with the centre.

Languages

Dubai is home to people from more than 200 nationalities, so this guide is being translated into the ten languages most spoken by UAE residents: English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Bengali, Tagalog, Russian, Chinese and Persian.
